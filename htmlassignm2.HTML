Reading has been an important part of human culture for thousands of years, evolving alongside changes in technology, society, and education. Early forms of reading were limited to handwritten texts and were often only available to scholars, religious leaders, and wealthy individual. Another growing trend is the popularity of audiobooks.

In recent years, reading has continued to change as digital technology has become more common. E-books, audiobooks, and reading apps have made it possible to access books almost anywhere.
