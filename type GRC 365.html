The Lantern in the Woods

Once upon a time, a young traveler wandered into a forest just as the sun began to disappear. The trees stretched high above her, and the winding paths seemed to lead nowhere. She carried a small lantern, but its light was barely enough to illuminate the ground beneath her feet.

She wished she could see the entire road ahead. Instead, she stood still, afraid of taking the wrong turn. Then an old owl called from a branch above her.

“You do not need to see the whole forest — only enough light to take the next step.”

The traveler continued walking. Every step revealed something new: a stream, a field of wildflowers, and finally a clearing filled with stars.She realized that the darkness had not hidden her destination. It had simply made the journey a mystery.

The Moral

Sometimes, finding your way means trusting the little light you already have.
